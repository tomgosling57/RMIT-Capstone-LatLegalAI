
### **1. Global Design System (The "LatLegal AI" Theme)**
* **Color Palette:** * **Background:** Deep Midnight Black/Navy (`#05070A`).
    * **Primary Accent:** High-contrast Golden Yellow (`#FFD700`).
    * **Secondary Accent:** Royal Blue (`#0033AA`).
    * **Surface/Cards:** Slate Gray/Blue (`#2A2E3E`).
* **Typography:** Modern Sans-serif (Inter or Roboto). Titles are Uppercase with generous tracking.
* **UI Components:**
    * **Navbar:** Dark background with a thin blue bottom border. Logo on the left, "Home, Upload, Pricing, About" links in the center, and a rounded "Sign Up!" button in yellow on the far right.
    * **Buttons:** Primary buttons are solid yellow with black text and rounded corners.
    * **Inputs:** Dark slate background with subtle rounded corners.

---

### **2. Page Specifications**

#### **Page A: Homepage (Landing)**
* **Content:** A centered hero section with a brief value proposition: "Explain what the web application is and what it is used for."
* **Layout:** Clean, minimal, focused on a clear call to action (CTA).

#### **Page B: Authentication (Login/Sign-Up)**
* **Header:** Large "AUTHENTICATION" title.
* **Tabs:** Toggle between "Login" and "Sign Up" separated by a vertical divider.
* **Form:** "User" and "Password" fields with a large yellow "Login" button.
* **Social Auth:** An "Or" separator followed by rounded "Google" and "Facebook" OAuth buttons with brand icons.

#### **Page C: Pricing**
* **Header:** "PRICING" title with the sub-headline "Choose the plan that suits you best!".
* **Grid:** Three vertical cards.
* **Interactive State:** The selected card should feature a bright yellow border highlight.
* **Card Content:** Includes the plan name (e.g., "Free"), a central feature list area, and a dark action button at the bottom.

#### **Page D: Document Upload**
* **Header:** "Upload Document" with the sub-text "Upload any legal document to get started."
* **Control:** A wide, dark-gray upload bar containing a yellow "Choose" button on the left.

#### **Page E: Summarized View (Dashboard)**
* **Layout:** Three-card grid system.
    * **Left Column:** A tall, large card for the main summary text.
    * **Right Column:** Two stacked smaller cards for specific AI-generated metadata or extracted sections.
* **Footer/Action:** A persistent upload bar at the bottom to allow for quick re-uploads.
